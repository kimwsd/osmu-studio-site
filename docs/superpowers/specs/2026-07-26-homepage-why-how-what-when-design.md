# OSMU STUDIO Homepage Narrative Redesign

Date: 2026-07-26
Status: Approved design

## Objective

Reorganize the OSMU STUDIO homepage around the strategic logic of Why, How, What, and When without exposing those four words as formulaic section labels.

The page should feel like a polished branding-agency presentation rather than a framework diagram. A visitor should understand:

1. why OSMU STUDIO exists;
2. how the studio approaches brand problems;
3. what services and work it delivers;
4. when a business should contact the studio.

The primary conversion is a qualified project inquiry. Portfolio exploration is the secondary conversion.

## Brand Positioning

OSMU STUDIO is a branding agency, not an interior design or construction company. It connects brand strategy to identity, space branding, marketing, and brand film as one customer-experience system.

The existing slogan remains the central expression:

> One Source. Multi Use.
> We give brands a reason to be remembered.

## Narrative Principles

- Do not display `Why`, `How`, `What`, or `When` as large section headings.
- Let page order and copy answer those questions naturally.
- Keep the current monochrome, editorial, minimal visual language.
- Reduce repetition between Value Proposition, Case Study, OSMU Framework, and Why OSMU.
- Show portfolio proof early enough to establish credibility.
- Use English for concise strategic headlines and Korean for clarity and persuasion.
- Preserve the direct, professional tone. Avoid vague agency language and unsupported claims.

### 2. Our Point of View

Purpose: Explain the strategic belief behind the studio and replace duplicated Value Proposition and Why OSMU content.

Section title:

> Our Point of View

Headline:

> A clear reason makes every touchpoint stronger.

Korean statement:

> 하나의 명확한 이유가 브랜드의 모든 접점을 강하게 만듭니다.

Three principles:

1. **Clarify the reason**
   시장과 고객을 읽고 브랜드가 존재해야 할 이유와 선택의 기준을 선명하게 정의합니다.
2. **Connect the experience**
   아이덴티티, 공간, 콘텐츠와 마케팅이 하나의 브랜드 언어로 이어지게 합니다.
3. **Make it work**
   결과물이 발표 자료에 머무르지 않고 실제 운영과 고객의 다음 행동에서 작동하도록 설계합니다.

### 3. Selected Portfolio

Purpose: Provide visual proof before asking visitors to study the full methodology.

Requirements:

- Preserve the existing image and video card renderer.
- Preserve equal card media ratios.
- Preserve category filters and counts.
- Preserve direct links to project detail pages.
- Keep `포트폴리오 전체 보기` as the closing link.

### 4. Our Approach

Purpose: Explain how OSMU STUDIO turns one brand idea into a connected system. This section replaces the separate Case Study and OSMU Framework sections.

Section title:

> Our Approach

Headline:

> One idea. One system. Every touchpoint.

Korean statement:

> 하나의 브랜드 본질을 발견하고, 고객이 만나는 모든 장면으로 확장합니다.

Four stages:

1. **Define**
   비즈니스와 시장, 고객을 읽고 브랜드가 반드시 가져야 할 이유와 기준을 정의합니다.
2. **Design**
   전략을 아이덴티티와 공간, 콘텐츠에서 알아볼 수 있는 브랜드 경험으로 번역합니다.
3. **Connect**
   각 접점이 서로 다른 결과물이 아니라 같은 방향을 말하는 하나의 시스템이 되도록 연결합니다.
4. **Activate**
   브랜드가 실제 운영과 캠페인, 고객 행동에서 지속적으로 작동하도록 적용하고 확장합니다.

Visual treatment:

- Retain the dark background currently used by OSMU Framework.
- Use four balanced columns on desktop and a single vertical sequence on mobile.
- Use existing reveal motion only.

## Responsive Behavior

### Desktop

- Keep the existing editorial grid, full-width rules, and restrained whitespace.
- Use two-column narrative intros where the current layout supports them.
- Use three or four equal columns only when each item has comparable content weight.

### Mobile

- Stack all narrative content in reading order.
- Keep Korean body copy at a comfortable legible size.
- Keep service controls and CTAs at touch-friendly dimensions.
- Avoid horizontal overflow in headings, filters, and counters.
- Preserve equal media ratios for portfolio image and video cards.

## Interaction

- Keep the hero typewriter sequence.
- Keep section reveal animations.
- Keep the service ticker.
- Keep portfolio filtering and card links.
- Keep accordion behavior for services and FAQ.
- Do not add pinned-scroll or high-cost animation.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## SEO, AEO, GEO, and Schema Constraints

- Keep exactly one semantic H1.
- Keep titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, robots directives, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata intact unless copy changes require a matching description update.
- Keep Organization, WebSite, FAQPage, ItemList, Breadcrumb, Service, and CreativeWork JSON-LD valid.
- Update visible FAQ copy and FAQPage schema together if either changes.
- Keep `home.html` and `index.html` functionally identical.
- Preserve crawlable links to all five static service pages and all portfolio pages.
- Preserve `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, and `llms.txt`.

## Implementation Boundaries

In scope:

- Homepage section order and content.
- Homepage-specific styling needed for the approved narrative.
- Homepage link and interaction adjustments.
- Matching homepage structured-data copy when visible claims change.
- Desktop and mobile validation.

Out of scope:

- Redesigning service, studio, portfolio, project, admin, privacy, or terms pages.
- Changing Supabase data structures.
- Rebuilding the portfolio renderer.
- Adding new external libraries or animation frameworks.
- Changing the established logo, navigation, footer, or Kakao integration.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A first-time visitor can identify the studio's purpose, approach, services, project proof, and ideal inquiry timing from one homepage pass.
- The duplicated Value Proposition, Case Study, OSMU Framework, and Why OSMU arguments are consolidated.
- Portfolio cards, filters, and links continue to work.
- Every service points to its canonical service page.
- The new inquiry-moment section appears on desktop and mobile.
- Existing typewriter, ticker, reveal, accordion, counter, and FAQ interactions continue to work.
- Homepage structured data remains valid and consistent with visible content.
- `home.html` and `index.html` remain synchronized.
- Existing relevant regression tests pass, with new tests added for the narrative order and canonical service links.
